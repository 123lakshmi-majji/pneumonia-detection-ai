Our Story

About PneuVision AI

Bridging AI and radiology to save lives.

Our Mission

To empower clinicians worldwide with fast, accurate, and explainable AI for pneumonia detection – reducing diagnostic delays and improving patient outcomes.

Our Vision

A future where AI‑augmented diagnostics are accessible in every healthcare setting, from tertiary hospitals to rural clinics.

Why Pneumonia?

Pneumonia kills over 2.5 million people annually – many deaths could be prevented with timely diagnosis. Chest X‑ray interpretation is expert‑dependent and often delayed.

Our Solution

PneuVision AI delivers instant, explainable pneumonia classification (bacterial / viral / normal) using a ResNet50 deep learning model, validated on thousands of radiographs.
