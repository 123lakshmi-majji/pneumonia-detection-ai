ResNet50 • Grad‑CAM

Clinical Deep Learning for Pneumonia

Transfer learning from ImageNet to chest X‑ray classification — explainable, validated, and ready for clinical decision support.

Radiological Phenotypes

The model distinguishes three classes based on visual patterns recognised by ResNet50.

ClassKey Imaging SignsTypical Presentation
NormalClear lung fields, intact vascular markings, no infiltratesAsymptomatic or incidental finding
BacterialLobar consolidation, air bronchograms, possible pleural effusionSudden high fever, productive cough, leukocytosis
ViralDiffuse interstitial or ground‑glass opacities, peribronchial thickeningGradual onset, dry cough, myalgia, often seasonal
Grad‑CAM attention maps consistently highlight the lung zones where these patterns appear, aligning with radiological expertise.

ResNet50 – Custom Head

Base model

ResNet50 pre‑trained on ImageNet (1.4M images, 1000 classes). The convolutional base is frozen during the first training phase to leverage transfer learning; later fine‑tuning unlocks the top layers.

Custom classification head

GlobalAveragePooling2D → Dense(256, ReLU, L2 regularisation) → Dropout(0.5) → Dense(3, softmax). This lightweight head adapts generic features to pneumonia‑specific patterns.

Input preprocessing

All X‑ray images are resized to 224×224×3 and preprocessed using tf.keras.applications.resnet50.preprocess_input (mean subtraction and scaling).

Explainable AI – Gradient‑weighted CAM

Every prediction includes a heatmap overlay that highlights the X‑ray regions that most influenced the decision.

  • Uses the last convolutional block of ResNet50 (conv5_block3_out) to generate spatial activation maps.
  • Red = high contribution to the predicted class; blue = low.
  • Helps clinicians verify that the model focuses on pathological lung zones (e.g., consolidations, interstitial patterns).
